DATE AND TIME FUNCTIONS
-------------------------------------------------------

CURDATE()-->>CURRENT DATE
CURTIME()-->>CURRENT TIME
NOW()-->> CURRENT DATE AND TIME
DATE():-EXTRACTED DATE PART
TIME():-EXTRACTED TIMEPART
YEAR():-EXTRACT YEAR
MONTH():-EXTRACT MONTH
DAY()/DAYOFMONTH():EXTRACT DAY OF MONTH
WEEK():-EXTRACT WEEK NUMBER
DAYOFWEEK():-EXTRACT DAY OF THE WEEK
DATE_ADD():-ADD TIME TO A DATE
DATE_SUB():-SUB TIME FROM A DATE
DATEDIFF():-DIFFERENCE BETWEEN TWO DATES
TIMESTAMPDIFF():-DIFFERENCE BETWEEN TWO DATEIME VALUES
