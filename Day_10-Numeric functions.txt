Numeric functions:
-------------------------------------
1. abs():-absolute value of a number
2. ceil():-round up
3. floor():-round down
4. round():-round to a specific number of digits
5. mod():-modulo of a number
5. pow()/power():-exponentiation
6. sqrt():-square root value
7. EXP():-Exponential function.
7. RADIANS():-convert degree to radians
8. DEGREE():-convert radians to degrees
9. SIGN():-returns the sign of a number
10. RAND():-generate a random number
11. GREATEST():-find the max value
12. LEAST():-find the min value
13. HEX():-convert to hexadecimal
14. OCT():-convert to octal
15. BIN():-convert to binary
